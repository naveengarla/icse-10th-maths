# Chapter 3 — Shares and Dividends
## Start here

**New to this chapter? Open [01. Foundations](./foundations.md) first.** Follow the numbered contents below. You do not need to work out the order from the file names.

Keep this page open as your chapter guide. On a computer, **Ctrl-click** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd-click** (Mac) opens a lesson in a new tab; on a phone, press and hold a link and choose “Open in new tab”.

Study one topic at a time: read the explanation, explain why the method works, cover the solution and reproduce the exam steps, then attempt its checkpoint or transfer question on paper. You can split a step across several sessions.

## Table of contents — recommended learning order

1. **[Foundations — skills you need before shares](./foundations.md)**
   - Percentages and choosing the correct base.
   - Unitary method and reversing a calculation.
   - Increase, decrease and remaining amounts.
   - Translating words into equations; units and time.
   - Successive percentage changes and eight readiness checks.
2. **[Concepts — understand the vocabulary](./concepts.md)**
   - Face/nominal value and market value.
   - At par, premium and discount.
   - Investment, share count, dividend and annual income.
   - Return/yield, sale proceeds, capital gain and reinvestment.
3. **[Formulae — learn the reasons, then the rearrangements](./formulae.md)**
   - Buying shares; dividend per share; total dividend; yield.
   - Choosing a formula from the unknown.
   - Extra income, transactions and allocation equations.
   - Percentage bases and a formula recall check.
4. **[Read a problem — understand before calculating](./read-problems.md)**
   - Mark the givens, identify the unknown and decode the wording.
   - Plan direct, reverse, transaction and algebra problems.
   - Present given → method → substitution → answer with units → check.
5. **[Understand and buy shares](./understanding.md)**
   - Decode quoted prices, premiums and discounts.
   - Move between investment and number of shares.
   - Detailed exam models: [E1 — decode price](./worked-solutions.md#e1-decode-the-price-before-finding-cost) and [E2 — investment to income](./worked-solutions.md#e2-investment-to-shares-to-annual-income).
   - Attempt the lesson checkpoint before moving on.
6. **[Dividend and income](./income.md)**
   - Start with dividend on one share.
   - Find income, dividend rate, required investment and additional shares.
   - Detailed exam models: [E3 — dividend rate](./worked-solutions.md#e3-find-the-companys-dividend-rate), [E4 — required investment](./worked-solutions.md#e4-desired-income-to-required-investment) and [E6 — additional income](./worked-solutions.md#e6-additional-income-means-additional-shares).
7. **[Return and yield](./yield.md)**
   - Distinguish dividend percentage from return on money invested.
   - Reverse a yield calculation and compare investments.
   - Detailed exam models: [E5 — yield and reverse calculations](./worked-solutions.md#e5-yield-and-its-reverse-calculations) and [E7 — comparison](./worked-solutions.md#e7-compare-investments-fairly).
   - Attempt the independent checkpoint.
8. **[Selling and reinvesting](./transactions.md)**
   - Separate purchase cost, sale proceeds, capital gain and dividend.
   - Follow money into the new investment; compare old and new income.
   - Detailed exam models: [E8 — partial sales](./worked-solutions.md#e8-partial-sales-cost-proceeds-and-what-remains), [E9 — reinvestment](./worked-solutions.md#e9-sell-and-reinvest-money-is-the-connecting-quantity), [E10 — dividend included](./worked-solutions.md#e10-reinvest-the-dividend-too-and-choose-the-percentage-base) and [E11 — reverse reinvestment](./worked-solutions.md#e11-reverse-reinvestment-build-the-equation-per-old-share).
9. **[Multiple-company problems](./allocation.md)**
   - Choose equations for equal investments, equal incomes or combined income.
   - Handle allocation, a third company and case-study data.
   - Detailed exam models: [E12 — equal investments](./worked-solutions.md#e12-equal-investments-sum-and-difference-are-different-conditions), [E13 — equal dividend](./worked-solutions.md#e13-equal-dividend-unknown-rate-versus-unknown-allocation) and [E14 — three companies](./worked-solutions.md#e14-three-companies-find-the-missing-income-before-overall-yield).
   - Attempt the independent checkpoint.
10. **[Mixed recognition — choose the method yourself](./recognition.md)**
    - Identify the problem type before doing arithmetic.
    - Recognise missing information, ambiguous wording and exam traps.
    - Detailed exam model: [E15 — wording, assertion–reason and case studies](./worked-solutions.md#e15-source-wording-statement-reasoning-and-case-studies).
11. **[Core practice — attempt independently](./practice.md)**
    - Work from top to bottom: 29 questions / 52 subparts cover the 48 mapped variations.
    - Each question links back to relevant teaching if you need help.
    - Attempt on paper before opening the answer; write the reasoning and exam steps.
12. **[Strengthen — extra practice where you need it](./strengthen.md)**
    - Use the variation lookup to find questions matching a mistake.
    - Revisit the explanation, solve another question, then retry the original without help.
    - This is an as-needed detour during Core; you do not need to finish the entire bank before Exam.
13. **[Exam practice — mix, time and review](./exam.md)**
    - Try the suggested 35-minute paper with 25 practice marks.
    - Continue with mixed challenges, MCQs, assertion–reason and the case study.
    - Check formula recall and review errors afterwards. The marks are suggested practice marks; these are textbook exam-style questions, not verified PYQs.

## Where should I resume?

- **First visit:** start at step 1.
- **Learning a topic:** continue steps 5–9, using the linked worked examples and checkpoints.
- **Understand lessons but struggle to choose a method:** revisit step 10, then attempt Core.
- **Made a mistake in Core:** use Strengthen for that skill, then return to your place in Core.
- **Can solve Core independently:** move to Exam.

Keep a short paper record: **last step completed / last question attempted / skill to revisit**. This makes the next study session easy to start.
