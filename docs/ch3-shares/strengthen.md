Textbook p. 25; PDF page 2. Difficulty 1/3.

Find the cost of (i) 350 ₹20 shares at ₹7 premium; (ii) 275 ₹60 shares at ₹10 discount; (iii) 50 ₹40 shares quoted at ₹38.50.

<details>
<summary>Method and solution — open after attempting</summary>

**iii:** The quote is M = ₹38.50; I = 50 × 38.50 = ₹1,925.

</details>

## W12

Textbook p. 30; PDF page 7. Difficulty 3/3.

Invest ₹8,000 in 7% ₹100 shares at ₹80. After one year sell at ₹75 and reinvest sale proceeds plus dividend in 18% ₹25 shares at ₹41. Find both years’ incomes and increase in return on the original investment.

**Source note:** Continues on printed p. 31. The source uses “percentage increase in return” with the explicit original-investment denominator.

<details>
<summary>Method and solution — open after attempting</summary>

**i:** Old n = 100; first dividend = 100 × 7 = ₹700.

**ii:** New capital = 100 × 75 + 700 = ₹8,200; new n = 200; second income = 200 × 4.5 = ₹900.

**iii:** Increase relative to original capital = (900 − 700)/8000 × 100 = 2.5% of original capital, a 2.5 percentage-point increase in annual income/original-capital ratio. Relative income growth is 200/700 × 100 = 28.571…%, a different question.

</details>
